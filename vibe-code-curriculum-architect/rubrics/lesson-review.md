# Lesson Review Rubric

Check each item and cite evidence:

- The objective is observable.
- Prerequisites are explicit and already taught.
- The mental model precedes implementation detail.
- The demo is small enough to inspect.
- The lab has a visible output and success criteria.
- Common errors have a recovery path.
- The assessment checks understanding, not only completion.
- The lesson advances the capstone.
- Language and examples are accessible to non-technical learners.

Return critical issues first, then warnings and one or two high-value improvements.
