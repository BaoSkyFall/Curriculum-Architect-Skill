# Research Policy Reference

## Required Evidence

For a freshness-sensitive decision, collect:

- official product or API documentation;
- current release or changelog evidence;
- repository or maintainer activity when relevant;
- pricing, access, and platform constraints;
- known breaking changes, deprecations, or migration notes;
- at least one viable alternative.

## Record Facts Separately From Decisions

`TECH_RADAR.md` records what is current and how confident the evidence is. `DECISIONS.md` records why the curriculum uses, defers, or rejects the option.

## Freshness

Always include `last_verified`. Mark stale entries for re-verification instead of silently reusing them. If research cannot be completed, state the gap and choose the least brittle teaching path.
